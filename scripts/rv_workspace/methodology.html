Version 3 · gross package basis points · 5bp minimum opportunity. One signal list provides evidence for discretionary trading decisions. One historical qualification screen applies; there is no investigate/paper hierarchy.

Universe and construction

246 structures and 614 model specifications across USD SOFR, EUR Euribor, ESTR OIS, GBP SONIA, JPY TONA, AUD BBSW, NZD IRS, CHF SARON, KRW CD, INR OIS, SGD SORA and THB IRS. USD/EUR cross-market comparisons use ESTR.

  • Every family: 1s2s, 1s3s, 2s5s, 2s10s, 5s10s; 1s2s5s, 1s3s5s, 2s5s10s, 3s5s10s; and calculated forward spreads 1y1y/2y1y, 2y1y/3y2y, 3y2y/5y5y.
  • Retained long-end structures: 10s30s and 5s10s30s in USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, NZD and CHF.
  • Eight pairings: USD–EUR (ESTR), USD–GBP, USD–JPY, USD–AUD, USD–KRW, AUD–NZD, SGD–THB, AUD–KRW.
  • Each pairing: differences between 2s5s, 2s10s, 5s10s, 2s5s10s and 3s5s10s; differences between four forward curves (1y1y/2y1y, 2y1y/3y2y, 1y1y/5y5y, 3y2y/5y5y); and outright 3y2y and 5y5y forward differences.

A curve is long-tenor minus short-tenor. A fly is 2 × belly − short wing − long wing. Cross-market quote order is printed explicitly in each review. Long quote means positioning for that package quote to rise; short quote means positioning for it to fall. These coefficients are approximate yield-risk exposures, not executable notional hedges.

All forwards are calculated from same-family, same-date cached spot swaps using F(s,t) = ((s+t) × S(s+t) − s × S(s)) / t: 1y1y = 2S2−S1; 2y1y = 3S3−2S2; 3y2y = (5S5−3S3)/2; 5y5y = 2S10−S5. No quoted-forward splicing, discount-curve bootstrapping or gap filling. This provides consistent approximations, not sub-basis-point pricing.

Three models

  1. Mean reversion: compares the package quote with its trailing mean. The residual is quote minus mean.
  2. Conditioned: estimates the quote expected from related curve inputs. Local slopes condition on their average rate; flies on wing level and wing slope; local forward curves on average forward rate; cross-market shapes on the anchor shape and relative rate level. Outright cross-market forwards use only the anchor forward, avoiding a tautological regressor.
  3. PCA: extracts two main curve factors. Factor scores use the other curve nodes to reconstruct the targeted node(s), so the current target does not explain itself. Used for spot curves/flies only; forwards and cross-market structures have mean and conditioned models.

Primary fits use 504 past observations and refit every five observations. No current/future observation enters its own fit. Residual sigma must be at least 0.5bp for an estimable model; missing or ill-conditioned fits are held and remain visible.

Single historical qualification screen

A model must have at least 8 completed full-history trades, at least 3 completed trades since 1 January 2022, and strictly positive average gross outcomes in both periods. Its proposed direction must also have a strictly positive full-history average gross outcome. Directional trade counts are shown without a separate minimum-count hurdle; absent directional evidence cannot pass. Exactly zero does not pass. There are no win-rate, confidence-interval, parameter-sensitivity or cost-stress gates.

These checks use completed reference-strategy trades available at the scan. They qualify current candidates; the reference backtest itself is unchanged and is not presented as a backtest of a historically screened portfolio. A versioned selection ledger records the first actual admission under this policy. Existing raw historical episodes are not retrospectively assumed to have passed the screen.

Entry and exit

In addition to passing the historical screen, an entry needs 1.5 ≤ |z| < 3.5 and at least 5 gross bp to the 0.25-sigma exit target per whole package. This is the move to the exit target, not merely the gap to fair value. No execution costs or adjustable cost scenarios are subtracted.

For direction d (+1 long quote, −1 short quote), target = fair − d×0.25σ and opportunity = d×(target−quote). Adverse stop = fair − d×3.5σ. Entry-fit coefficients and sigma remain frozen through the trade; conditioned/PCA fair value can still change as their inputs move. Time exits are approximately 40 observations, capped around 65 calendar days. There is a five-observation cooldown.

Backtest and evidence

Each historical signal uses information available at that close, applies the 5bp floor on that signal date and fills at the next observation’s close. Exit signals also fill next close. Long data gaps can cancel pending entries. Gross P&L is direction × change in package quote; it excludes costs, carry, roll and currency conversion. Portfolio returns and executable notionals are not inferred from these bp results.

The review shows full history, entry cohorts since 2022 and since 2025, with long/short directions separately: completed trades, win rate, average/median gross outcome, adverse excursion, holding period, and worst trade. Full-strategy drawdown includes daily marks and any open model episode. The trade counts and gross means feed the single screen above; other statistics remain evidence for discretion.

Research runs also test a 252-observation window and a 2-sigma entry, plus an unfiltered diagnostic without the 5bp floor. A 40-observation circular block bootstrap uses 200,000 draws for a 90% interval on mean daily gross bp, including inactive days. BY adjustment uses a conservative family count of 2,456 planned comparisons. Displayed adjusted values apply to primary models; sensitivities are descriptive. These are exploratory results, not an untouched holdout or a performance-selected portfolio. Daily scans do not recompute research diagnostics.

New today, tracking and current quotes

Historical model-trigger dates, first-observation dates, simulated fills and current cached indications are separate. Previously admitted signals continue to be tracked, with any subsequent historical-screen failure clearly flagged, when their remaining opportunity drops below 5bp, until convergence, stop or time exit. Search retains closed histories and structures with no current signal.

Eligible current cached indications can be acted on immediately after the scan; there is no artificial next-day wait. This requires matching spot tickers, same-day cache acquisition within 60 minutes and no more than five minutes of timestamp skew. File acquisition time is not an exchange quote timestamp. When matching snapshots are absent, the workspace explicitly shows last-close discoveries and simulated historical tracking; it does not call them live entries.

The refresh gate requires a successful dated dashboard refresh; a weekend refresh is accepted after Friday. Histories must reach the required previous-weekday close. Missing/stale inputs hold the affected models. Historical source revisions hold existing records rather than silently rewriting their trades.

Charts and older work

The gross strategy curve is from the actual rolling historical simulation. The quote/fair-value and z charts apply the latest frozen fit retrospectively and are explicitly labelled diagnostic; they must not be read as an out-of-sample fair-value forecast history.

Legacy v2 model research and the original six-candidate study remain dated, cost-inclusive archives, accessible from the resource links. Their trades are not migrated into v3 results or treated as your holdings.

Comparing the signal table

MR means mean reversion; CO means conditioned. Method, currency and agreement controls filter the display only; they do not change qualification. Hit rates and average gross outcomes are calculated per model from completed trades in the proposed direction, for the selected evidence period (since 2022 by default). Sample size n stays beside each hit rate; absent evidence is shown as unavailable, not zero. Same-direction agreement counts all currently qualifying methods, including methods hidden by the filter. Opposing qualified views remain flagged. These models share source data, so agreement is corroboration, not independent statistical significance.

Default ordering puts agreement first and then the lowest displayed model average gross outcome. Other sorts use the lowest displayed model hit rate, average or opportunity; model results are never averaged. Ten matching groups are shown initially, with an option to show all. Quote freshness remains in the banner and review; current cached rows are labelled individually when available. Historical trigger dates and simulated model lifecycle remain in the review.
