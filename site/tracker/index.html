Methodology and timing

Universe: UST 10Y, Bund 10Y, JGB 10Y, INR 5Y IRS and KRW 5Y IRS. The score combines 10/30, 20/60 and 40/120-day EMA differences, each divided by its trailing 252-day standard deviation and mapped through tanh(z/2). Their average contributes 85%; signs of yield changes over 189, 220, 252, 285 and 315 observations contribute 15%.

Primary pullback: overall score and slow component exceed +0.2, yields move below the 20-day EMA, then cross back above it within 20 observations while the trend persists. Reverse for receivers. Exit when the score crosses zero against the trade, or after 126 observations. Established trend enters beyond ±0.6 with the same exits. Breakout enters beyond the previous 55-day yield high/low and exits through the opposite 20-day range or at 126 observations. All require a flat decision day after exit.

Immediate alerts use the unchanged model state at the latest completed close and the actual observation timestamp. An already active signal at activation is context, not a newly triggered trade. A signal first discovered late is recorded when seen; no earlier fill or return is assigned. Cache gaps or revisions hold alerts. The historical next-close reference ledger continues separately and is never treated as an immediate execution record.

Legacy paper performance uses lagged 63-day volatility sizing, nominal 10% annual risk per market and equal five-market weights. It is gross yield-change accounting with no costs or carry/roll. Retrospective pullback Sharpe was 0.85; a statistically reliable timing edge was not established.
