Rules, timing and evidence

Primary rule: score and slow trend above +0.2, pullback below the 20-day EMA, then recovery within 20 observations; mirrored for receivers. Exit on score crossing zero or after 126 observations. At least one flat decision day after exit.

Orders are recorded when observed and filled at an eligible subsequent cached close. A close predating first observation cannot earn a hindsight entry. Late exits remain pending until an eligible close. Risk resizing uses prior observations under a standing rule. The always-payer benchmark starts at the first eligible close after launch.

Only completed-day cache rows are read. Revisions to previously processed history halt that market for review; missing sources do not become zero risk. Weekday lag is a freshness hint, not an exchange holiday calendar. The app must be running and the PC available for scheduled checks; cached inputs must be refreshed by their existing workflow.

Research: five-market cost-free pullback Sharpe 0.85 (retrospective). Timing advantage not statistically established; KRW has only 13 completed historical trades. The earlier carry sensitivity is excluded from this ledger.
