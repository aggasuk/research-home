SA momentum entry study

Methods · 2026-09-11 · Research methods

11 September 2026 • cached data only • no production changes

Decision: retain momentum as a research candidate, but do not promote these rules into proven trade recommendations. Three frozen entry-to-exit strategies made positive historical price-based P&L after assumed trading costs. None established a statistically reliable timing advantage over a benchmark matched to average signed exposure. All three trailed passive positive-ticker exposure scaled to comparable realized volatility. Rates deserve the next implementation check; broad momentum does not yet justify a dashboard rebuild.

What was tested

29 cached instruments audited; 26 included in the main portfolio. Evaluation 2017–10 September 2026, with most series ending 9 September. Earlier data supplies warmup. Rules were specified before this run, but the history and score had already been examined in earlier dashboard research: this is not untouched out-of-sample evidence. No optimization or choice of the best holding period was performed.

Established trend enters above +0.6/below −0.6 and exits at zero. Breakout enters beyond the previous 55 closes and exits through the opposite 20-close channel. Pullback recovery waits for a 20-day EMA recovery within a favourable established trend, and exits at zero. All rules are symmetric, capped at 126 observations, and execute at the following close. The signal therefore cannot earn the return from its own decision close to execution close.

Each instrument uses lagged 63-day volatility sizing to a nominal 10% annual risk budget, with volatility floors. Instruments receive equal weights within each class and the four classes receive equal weights. This is a synthetic risk-normalized basket, not a funded account simulation. Core commodities consist only of gold and silver. Positive rates exposure means yields rising. Missing-market dates carry zero P&L under fixed weights.

Timing lift subtracts each instrument’s passive P&L at its average signed strategy exposure in the same period, including equivalent resize costs. This is an ex-post control for directional bias, not a deployable benchmark. The intervals condition on that fitted exposure. All three intervals cross zero; wider three-rule-adjusted intervals also cross zero. Full-period positive strategy P&L is not equivalent to positive timing edge.

Inference uses 2,000 synchronous circular 63-observation blocks of portfolio P&L, retaining cross-market co-movement. The three-rule 98.33% intervals account only for these three primary comparisons, not historical model development, asset selection or subgroup exploration. Confidence intervals do not resolve missing instrument economics or structural regime changes.

The broad portfolio is much stronger recently. For established trend and pullback recovery, estimated within-period timing lift is negative in 2017–19 and 2024–latest despite positive raw P&L. This cautions against attributing recent gains to entry timing. Missed upside is visible in the passive comparison; these data cannot measure missed fundamental opportunities because no valid historical idea list was used.

Removing any one market leaves broad historical P&L positive, but this is not a proof of alpha. Established trend’s leave-one-market-out annual returns range from 2.38% to 2.74%; breakout 1.36%–2.49%; pullback 1.49%–3.22%. Individual trend/pullback samples often contain only 11–25 completed trades and a few winning episodes contribute a large share of positive trade P&L. Cross-market trades are not independent. Trend and pullback daily portfolio P&Ls correlate 0.87, so they should not count as independent confirmations.

Your previously listed positions — research implications

  • KOSPI: pullback recovery is promising retrospectively, but only 15 completed full-period trades; the best three winners contribute about 71% of positive completed-trade P&L. This is not yet an entry recommendation.
  • Gold: trend earns money but shows almost no lift over its matched directional exposure (+0.04% annual at the single-instrument risk budget); raw profitability mostly fails to establish timing value. Pullback remains provisional.
  • TWSE: pullback/breakout are positive candidates in this retrospective sample; require the same implementation and forward validation rather than selecting them because they won here.
  • Paid UST10Y: trend/pullback have positive price-based lift estimates, but the yield-series economics are incomplete.
  • WTI: no conclusion from this run. Its cached CL1 generic series contains a negative observation; the existing log-price engine cannot handle it correctly. Do not treat its earlier dashboard momentum/exhaustion backtests as validated until the return series is repaired.

Data and combined-entry audit

  • Cache: all 29 series were found. Source files were read directly without importing the Bloomberg cache module (which has side effects). Hashes and coverage are recorded in cache-audit.json; no cache copies or API requests were made.
  • Commodity generics: WTI excluded for invalid log/percentage-price handling. Brent and copper retained only in separate instrument diagnostics, excluded from the main basket because roll construction is unverified. Copper history starts in 2021, so it also lacks the early evaluation period. Large moves elsewhere are flagged in the audit, not silently winsorized or removed.
  • FX/equities/rates: spot/index/yield series omit material tradable return components. FX volatility targeting can imply substantial notional exposure; funding and forwards matter. The 2bp one-way price cost / 0.25bp yield cost are assumptions, not broker estimates. All exposure changes, including daily risk resizing, are charged. Open trades remain marked to market rather than force-closed at the sample end.
  • Fair value: inspected EURUSD/shared FV code fits on the window ending at the current last date and publishes at most 160 fitted chart points. Earlier chart residuals use later observations’ fitted coefficients. They are not a historical sequence of contemporaneously available entry signals. Refit rolling models at each historical decision date before testing confirmation versus immediate entry; then account for source publication times/revisions.
  • Gold positioning: expanding z-scores are causal in the index, but COMEX positions are indexed by their reporting date and forward-filled; inspected code does not shift them to their actual release time. CFTC normally publishes Friday data referring to Tuesday positions. Rebuild with actual release dates before backtesting entry timing. CFTC release schedule.
  • KOSPI positioning: rolling transforms appear backward-looking, but exported history is weekly Friday resampling and underlying publication times are not validated. Reconstruct daily signals and release lags. Do not use the partial final week as a future-known Friday observation.

Recommended next work

  1. Keep these three rule definitions and the full universe recorded; do not optimize around the winning markets.
  2. Validate executable returns first for a small rates basket, including actual contracts/swaps, sizing, carry/roll and costs. KRW’s apparent strength is an exploratory finding, not grounds to test only KRW.
  3. Reconstruct one FX fair-value model using historical rolling fits and data available at the time. Compare the same opportunity set with immediate entry versus fixed momentum confirmation, including opportunities never entered.
  4. Once implementable, freeze one primary rule and a comparator for a forward paper record. Record signals, executable quotes, costs and rejected/missed entries. Accumulate independent trade episodes; a few months is not automatically enough to validate these slow strategies.
  5. Do not add automatic trade badges or alter live Setups on these results. Change the dashboard’s evidence wording only in a separately implemented production change.

Reproduction and validation

Files: protocol.md, audit_cache.py, run_study.py, check_study.py, cache-audit.json, results.json, instrument-results.csv, derived-trades.csv and derived-portfolio-*.csv. Source and protocol hashes are recorded. Run with the existing Python installation that supports pandas 3 / pyarrow and matplotlib. The bundled Codex Python lacks those optional libraries.

Checks passed: each strategy’s historical output is identical when future observations are withheld; a same-day price shock cannot change that day’s held exposure or execution cost; nonnegative costs and P&L identities hold; no immediate long/short reversal; all 29 source-cache hashes unchanged. No Bloomberg requests, production file writes, deployments or research-portal document downloads.
