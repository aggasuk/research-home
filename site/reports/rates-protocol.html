Rates paper-test protocol

Methods · 2026-09-11 · Research methods

User requested cache only and no costs. Retain all three original entry/exit rules and all five rates markets. Transaction costs are zero, including risk resizing. No parameter selection or production changes.

Primary: equal-weight UST10, JGB10, Bund10, INR5 IRS, KRW5 IRS, each with the original lagged 10% annual risk target. Use 2017–latest complete cached dates and the same three historical subperiods. Benchmark: unchanged mean signed exposure per market and period, with identical risk sizing, fitted retrospectively as a diagnostic. Report gross price/yield P&L, timing lift and block intervals, individual-market trade counts, long/short contributions and leave-one-market-out results. This remains a yield-change proxy.

Carry/roll sensitivity: use the desk's existing par-curve approximation for INR and KRW 5Y only, without importing the live cache/curve modules. Use the generic Curncy 1/2/3/5/10Y histories consistently; do not splice longer BGN quotes or mix government yields with swap curves. Curves aligned to signal dates using only past observations, at most seven calendar days old. No backward filling. Matched comparison starts when all five curve nodes exist. Current signal and risk positions remain frozen; zero-fill excluded carry dates in neither version.

For the payer-positive convention, estimated three-month carry/roll in equivalent bp is 100[y(4.75) - F(0.25,5)], with F(s,t)=[(s+t)y(s+t)-sy(s)]/t. Linear tenor interpolation; y(0.25) uses 1Y as an explicit short-end proxy because the cache lacks a matched short-end curve. Accrue the previous available close's estimate over elapsed calendar days/365/0.25. This is the existing desk approximation, not a dual-curve swap valuation, realized carry, or a reconstruction of each aging held swap. Show zero, one-half, full and double estimated carry/roll to expose model dependence, not optimize it. Apply carry only over intervals with fresh curves and no gap over seven calendar days. Compare yield-only and adjusted P&L on identical dates. In a flat par curve expected adjustment is zero; a steep positive curve penalizes payers and benefits receivers.

Use the same synchronous 63-observation circular block bootstrap (2,000 replications); show pointwise and three-rule-adjusted intervals. Subgroup and carry sensitivities are exploratory. No untouched holdout exists. Record cache hashes and validate timing and carry signs.
